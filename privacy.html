Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Scope and provider

Nuvio is operated by Tarik Zukic in Washington State, United States. This policy covers the Nuvio iOS app and nuviomm.com. It distinguishes financial records stored on your device from information processed by advertising, subscription, website, and support services. Version 2.7 introduces optional Nuvio Pro subscriptions; availability depends on the App Store release in your region.

2. Your financial records

Expenses, income, budgets, loans, categories, and settings are stored in local app files. Nuvio does not require an app account, connect to your bank, or send these financial entries to the developer, AdMob, or RevenueCat. Receipt recognition runs on your device; the app does not retain receipt images after processing. Review the recognized details before saving a financial entry. Exports, sharing, device backups, and copies you choose to create are controlled by you and the services you select.

3. Advertising

The free experience uses Google AdMob. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, crash information, and performance information for advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. The information available depends on device permissions and SDK configuration. Nuvio can request personalized ads. Where consent is required, the app presents Google’s consent choices before requesting ads. On iOS, access to the advertising identifier and tracking across other companies’ apps or websites requires your App Tracking Transparency authorization. You can decline or change the applicable consent choices; an advertising identifier is not available without that authorization, and ads may be limited or non-personalized. Use the app’s privacy choices and iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to review the available controls. These services do not receive your financial entries from Nuvio. An active Nuvio Pro subscription removes ads. See Google’s iOS SDK data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy, and Google ad settings.

4. Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes App Store payments; the developer does not receive your payment card details. Nuvio uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore access, and determine Pro entitlements. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user identifier, subscription products, transaction and purchase history, entitlement status, and service information such as app/platform version, locale, currency, and last contact with the SDK. This supports subscription operation and aggregate subscription/customer activity reporting. An anonymous identifier is not a promise that service data cannot identify a device or installation. Nuvio does not send financial entries or receipt photographs to RevenueCat. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Website and support

This website uses Google Analytics for page and device/browser usage information; Google may use cookies or similar technologies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process IP addresses and request logs. See Google’s partner-site information, the Analytics opt-out tool, and GitHub’s Privacy Statement. If you submit the contact form, your name, email, selected subject, device details, message, any attached images, site language, and submission time are sent through Discord to the developer. If delivery fails, you can choose an email link to open a draft in your mail app. Images must be attached to that email manually. See Discord’s Privacy Policy. Do not send financial records, receipts, passwords, or payment details in support messages.

6. Retention, security, and international processing

Local financial records remain until you delete them or remove the app, subject to backups or exports you control. Support messages are retained as needed to respond and manage support. Subscription, advertising, hosting, and analytics providers retain information under their own policies and applicable obligations. Providers may process information in the United States and other countries. We use reasonable safeguards but cannot guarantee absolute security or recover local records that you have not backed up.

7. Your choices and requests

You can edit, export, or delete local financial records using app tools. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Device privacy settings, Google ad settings, and browser controls offer additional choices. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, receive a copy of, restrict, or object to processing, or opt out of certain uses or appeal a request decision. Email the developer to exercise applicable rights or ask about advertising-related sharing. We may need information to verify and locate the relevant service record; do not send your financial records. You may also contact your local data protection authority. This policy does not claim that every right applies in every jurisdiction.

8. Children

Nuvio is a general-audience finance organizer and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support or a service used by Nuvio, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Changes

We update this policy when the app, services, or practices change. The date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated through the website, app, or App Store where appropriate.

10. Contact

For privacy questions and requests, email team@nuviomm.com or use the contact page. Provider: Tarik Zukic, Washington State, United States.
