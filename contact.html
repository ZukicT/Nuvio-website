    Quick answers

    A few useful things to know before you send.

    How do I export my data?+

    Settings → Data Management → Export Data. Your transactions export as a CSV file.

    Is my data secure?+

    Financial entries stay in local app storage. Ads and subscription services communicate with their providers; see our Privacy Policy for details.

    How do I create budgets?+

    Go to the Budget tab and tap "+" to create a new budget with your spending limit.

    Does it work offline?+

    Core expense, budget, and loan tracking works offline. Ads, purchases, and restoring subscriptions require an internet connection.
