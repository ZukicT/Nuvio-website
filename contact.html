Common Questions

How do I export my data?

Settings → Data Management → Export Data. Your transactions export as a CSV file.

Is my data secure?

Yes! Everything stays on your device. No servers, no cloud, no data collection.

How do I create budgets?

Go to the Budget tab and tap "+" to create a new budget with your spending limit.

Does it work offline?

Absolutely! Nuvio works 100% offline. Track your finances anywhere, anytime.
