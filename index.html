FAQ

A few things you might be wondering.

Support↗
What is Nuvio?+

Nuvio is an expense tracker and budget planner for iPhone and iPad. Log income and expenses, set category budgets, and track loans while building better money habits.

Is Nuvio free to download?+

Yes. Nuvio is free to download, and the free experience includes ads. Optional Nuvio Pro adds receipt scanning and ad removal. Check the app for availability and local pricing.

Do I need to connect my bank or create an account?+

No. You enter your own transactions. Choose your currency and starting balance, then start tracking without a bank connection or a Nuvio account.

Where are my financial records stored?+

Your financial entries are stored on your device. Advertising, subscriptions, website analytics, and support process other information as explained in the Privacy Policy.

How can Nuvio help me save money?+

Start by logging your expenses so you can see where your money goes. Set category budgets and check what is left before you spend. These small habits can help you find room to save; the amount depends on your income, spending, and choices.

How do I start tracking daily expenses?+

Choose your currency and starting balance, then add an expense with an amount and category. Keep logging purchases and review your overview regularly to understand your spending. You enter your transactions yourself, without connecting a bank account.
